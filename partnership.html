Why this partnership matters

Distribution is the beginning. Building local capability is the bigger ambition.

The opportunity presented to Lesotho is not simply to move products from one place to another. It is to establish a stronger market, create space for Basotho entrepreneurs and work towards a future where more production, skills and economic value can be developed locally.

LIF brought the market-development and local-industry vision forward, connecting Food Sock and Kingdom Foods around an opportunity for Lesotho. Each partner contributes a different capability to the shared journey.

“We want opportunity to reach communities, enterprise to grow, and more of the value created to remain in Lesotho.”

This direction reflects the Foundation’s wider mission: to help end poverty by expanding opportunity, enterprise, skills and self-reliance.

Partnership design & roles

Three partners. Clear responsibilities. A shared future.

The partnership works because each organisation contributes its own strengths. The responsibilities below describe the intended roles within the partnership; detailed operational arrangements remain subject to the applicable agreements.

01

Life Improvement Foundation

The originator and market-development driving force for the Lesotho initiative.

  • Conceived the Lesotho market-development and local-manufacturing initiative.
  • Brought Food Sock and Kingdom Foods together around the opportunity.
  • Leads local market entry, stakeholder engagement and relationship building.
  • Develops the distribution environment and opportunities for local resellers.
  • Works to connect community participation with the longer-term manufacturing vision.
  • May nominate Eco Solutions (Pty) Ltd as an operating partner, subject to the relevant arrangements.
02

Food Sock

Product, brand and commercial expertise supporting the market opportunity.

  • Retains responsibility for its intellectual property, formulations, brands and recipes.
  • Contributes marketing and commercial strategy.
  • Provides wholesale pricing and product-related commercial information to the relevant partners.
  • Provides technical product support within the agreed arrangements.
  • Supports the supply and distribution of its finished product range.
  • Contributes product knowledge to market development and customer education.
03

Kingdom Foods

Manufacturing expertise supporting production and packaging arrangements.

  • Acts as the manufacturing partner for Food Sock under the applicable manufacturing agreement.
  • Manufactures and packages Food Sock products in accordance with agreed specifications and responsibilities.
  • Brings food-production and manufacturing experience to the wider initiative.
  • Supports the partnership’s product-supply arrangements within its agreed role.
  • Contributes to discussions about the future manufacturing pathway for Lesotho.
  • Its other product responsibilities, including VitaKidz-related supply arrangements, should follow the applicable agreements.
The shared direction

What we want this partnership to make possible

Our ambition goes beyond products. It is about building the conditions for more Basotho people to participate in the economy and contribute to a stronger local industry.

🌱

Local participation

Work towards greater use of Lesotho-based resources, suppliers, farmers and entrepreneurs wherever feasible.

🤝

Enterprise for communities

Open pathways for resellers, retailers, informal traders and community businesses to participate.

🧰

Skills and jobs

Build towards local employment, practical skills and production capability as the manufacturing journey develops.

🇱🇸

Value in Lesotho

Work towards the ambition of products by Basotho, for Basotho and the world.

Journey to the manufacturing plant

From market development to a future manufacturing base in Lesotho.

The partnership includes a commitment to work towards establishing a manufacturing plant in Lesotho within one year after the launch of Phase One of the reseller programme. This is a future commitment, not a statement that the plant is already operating.

  • Build a viable local market through the Phase One reseller programme.
  • Develop a stronger network of retailers, traders and community entrepreneurs.
  • Work towards sourcing up to 70% of relevant products and resources locally, where feasible and suitable.
  • Work towards packing products in Lesotho as local capability and arrangements allow.
  • Create opportunities for employment, skills development and local suppliers as the plant develops.

Our long-term direction: a stronger local economy in which Basotho can participate in distribution, supply, production and the value chain around it.
