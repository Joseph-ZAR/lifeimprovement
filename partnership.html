The partnership

A partnership that builds industry, not just distribution.

Life Improvement Foundation (LIF) is driving a vision that goes beyond bringing products into Lesotho. The ambition is to build a pathway from market development and distribution towards local manufacturing, skills development and sustainable economic opportunity.

The partnership brings together different capabilities. LIF leads the Lesotho market-development and distribution effort. Food Sock contributes its intellectual property, products and commercial expertise. Kingdom Foods has a defined manufacturing role under the manufacturing agreement.

This model is intended to help create opportunities for Basotho entrepreneurs, workers, suppliers and communities while building the foundations for productive industry in Lesotho.

The ambition is bigger than moving products. It is about building local capability, creating jobs and keeping more value in Lesotho.

The proposed manufacturing facility is part of the future vision for the partnership. The facility shown on this page is conceptual imagery, not a claim that a completed factory is already operating.

Partnership design & roles

ROLES OF EACH PARTY IN THE PARTNERSHIP

Each party has a distinct responsibility. This structure recognises LIF's leadership of the Lesotho initiative, Food Sock's ownership of its intellectual property, and Kingdom Foods' manufacturing role.

1

Life Improvement Foundation

Life Improvement Foundation is the originator and driving force behind this tripartite partnership. Life Improvement Foundation conceived the Lesotho market development and local manufacturing initiative, and brought Food Sock and Kingdom Foods together. The investment is made in Lesotho and stays in Lesotho to benefit Basotho.

Life Improvement Foundation is the Lesotho market-development and distribution partner, facilitating market entry, managing stakeholder relationships, building sales infrastructure, and developing distribution channels.

Life Improvement Foundation may nominate Eco Solutions (Pty) Ltd as its operating partner.

2

Food Sock

Food Sock owns the intellectual property, product formulations, brands and recipes. Food Sock drives marketing and commercial strategy, sets wholesale pricing, and provides technical support.

Food Sock supplies its finished product range for distribution.

3

Kingdom Foods

Kingdom Foods is strictly the manufacturing partner. Kingdom Foods manufactures and packages Food Sock products under the manufacturing agreement.

Kingdom Foods also supplies VitaKids — its own milkshake sachet range — which is complementary, not competing.

🤝

This structure ensures Food Sock's IP is protected, manufacturing is localised in Lesotho, and Life Improvement Foundation leads the Lesotho benefit. Life Improvement Foundation's vision drives the entire venture, including the local manufacturing component with Kingdom Foods as manufacturing partner.
