A Partnership That Builds Industry, Not Just Distribution.

Life Improvement Foundation, Food Sock and Kingdom Foods are bringing complementary strengths together around a bigger opportunity for Lesotho: grow the market, develop local enterprise, create meaningful work and build a pathway towards manufacturing in our own country.

One partnership. One shared direction. Working towards enterprise, local capability and lasting impact.

Lesotho should not only be a destination for products. It should also be a place where value is created.

Our ambition is to help create the conditions for local enterprise, skills, jobs and a stronger manufacturing future.

The principle

Distribution is the beginning. Building local capability is the bigger ambition.

The opportunity presented to Lesotho is not simply to move products from one place to another. It is to establish a stronger market, create space for Basotho entrepreneurs and work towards a future where more production, skills and economic value can be developed locally.

Life Improvement Foundation brought forward the market-development and local-industry vision, connecting Food Sock and Kingdom Foods around an opportunity for Lesotho. Each partner contributes different capabilities to the shared journey.

The Foundation’s wider mission is to help end poverty by expanding opportunity, enterprise, skills and self-reliance. The partnership is intended to connect commercial activity with a longer-term ambition for local economic development.

“Products by Basotho, for Basotho and the world.”

Partnership design & roles

ROLES OF EACH PARTY IN THE PARTNERSHIP

The partnership works because each organisation contributes its own strengths. The following describes the intended roles within the partnership; detailed operational arrangements remain subject to the applicable agreements.

1

Life Improvement Foundation

The originator and driving force behind the Lesotho initiative.

Life Improvement Foundation conceived the Lesotho market development and local manufacturing initiative, and brought Food Sock and Kingdom Foods together.

The vision is for investment and value created through this initiative to benefit Lesotho and Basotho.

  • Leads local market development and distribution planning.
  • Facilitates market entry and stakeholder relationships.
  • Builds sales infrastructure and develops distribution channels.
  • Connects resellers and community participation with the wider vision.
  • Works to advance the local manufacturing ambition.
Additional operating arrangement

Life Improvement Foundation may nominate Eco Solutions (Pty) Ltd as its operating partner, subject to the relevant arrangements and agreements.

2

Food Sock

The brand, product intellectual property and commercial expertise.

Food Sock owns the intellectual property, product formulations, brands and recipes associated with its range.

  • Retains responsibility for its intellectual property, formulations, brands and recipes.
  • Drives marketing and commercial strategy.
  • Sets wholesale pricing and supplies relevant commercial information.
  • Provides technical support within the agreed arrangements.
  • Supplies its finished product range for distribution.
  • Contributes product knowledge to market development and customer education.
How this supports the partnership

Food Sock’s product and brand expertise supports market development while the partnership works towards expanding local capability in Lesotho.

3

Kingdom Foods

The manufacturing partner for Food Sock under the applicable agreement.

Kingdom Foods is the manufacturing partner for Food Sock products under the applicable manufacturing agreement.

  • Manufactures and packages Food Sock products under the manufacturing agreement.
  • Works to agreed product specifications and responsibilities.
  • Contributes manufacturing and production expertise.
  • Supports product-supply arrangements within its agreed role.
  • Supplies VitaKids, its own milkshake sachet range, as a complementary product range.
Complementary products

VitaKids is a Kingdom Foods product range that complements the wider offering. Product ownership and manufacturing responsibilities should be understood in accordance with the relevant agreements.

Journey to the manufacturing plant

From Market Development to a Future Manufacturing Base in Lesotho.

The partnership includes a commitment to work towards establishing a manufacturing plant in Lesotho within one year after the launch of Phase One of the reseller programme.

Phase One is intended to help build a stronger market and a nationwide network of resellers. This market-development work is part of the pathway towards local production and packaging.

1
Develop the local market Build awareness, distribution and participation through the reseller programme.
2
Connect local suppliers Work towards suitable supply relationships with Basotho farmers, millers and businesses.
3
Build production capability Work towards local manufacturing, packaging, employment and skills development.
4
Expand the opportunity Develop a stronger local value chain with potential to serve markets beyond Lesotho.
Important distinction: the manufacturing plant shown here is a conceptual vision, not an existing operating facility. The timeline describes the partnership commitment and ambition, not a claim that construction or production has already begun.

Local value creation

More Local Sourcing. More Local Participation.

The partnership’s ambition is to source up to 70% of relevant ingredients and resources locally when manufacturing develops, where feasible and subject to quality, availability, food-safety requirements and commercial arrangements.

This could create supply opportunities for Basotho farmers, millers, suppliers and entrepreneurs while supporting local processing, packaging, employment and skills development.

The goal is to build a supply chain that strengthens local participation and keeps more economic value circulating in Lesotho.

Local-sourcing ambition

A target for sourcing relevant ingredients and resources from local suppliers as manufacturing develops, where suitable and commercially feasible.
