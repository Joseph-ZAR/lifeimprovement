Our shared purpose

Let Us Build This Opportunity Together

Fellow Basotho, this is an invitation to take part in an ambitious journey: strengthening our food supply, supporting local enterprise and creating more ways for people to earn a living.

Through the LIF, Food Sock and Kingdom Foods initiative, we want to connect communities with products, distribution opportunities and a longer-term vision for manufacturing in Lesotho.

Every participating farmer, reseller, vendor, cooperative and community ambassador can help grow a stronger network. Food sovereignty starts with participation.

Keep more value in Lesotho

Build a Stronger Local Food Economy

Our longer-term ambition is to expand local sourcing as manufacturing develops. The programme aims for at least 70% of relevant ingredients to be sourced locally when the plant is established, subject to availability, quality, food-safety requirements and commercial agreements.

This creates an opportunity to connect Basotho farmers, millers, suppliers, workers and entrepreneurs with a developing value chain.

A pathway to participation

How the Reseller Opportunity Is Intended to Work

The programme is designed to help participants understand the steps from joining to selling and, where applicable, earning programme benefits.

Programme step How it is intended to work
Join the programme Register your interest and receive details of available starter options, product information and trading requirements.
Sell individual sachets Purchase through the approved channel and resell according to the applicable reseller terms. Actual earnings depend on costs, selling terms and completed sales.
Explore bulk products Ask about available bulk formats and their applicable reseller terms.
Earn programme points The proposed points model is one point for every M100 in eligible sales, subject to programme rules and verification.
Redeem eligible points Points may be redeemable for discounts on future orders if the final programme terms provide for this benefit.
Future share opportunities Eligible participants may be invited to consider future share opportunities if a manufacturing business is established and a lawful offering becomes available. This is not guaranteed.
Important: Final eligibility rules, points, discounts, starter options and any future investment opportunity must be confirmed in official programme terms. Points do not automatically represent shares or an ownership interest.
