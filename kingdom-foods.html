Kingdom Foods: A New Chapter in Lesotho’s Industrial Journey

Kingdom Foods is now part of the Life Improvement Foundation (LIF) family. Under this partnership, Kingdom Foods products will be resold in Lesotho, connecting local consumers and entrepreneurs with the brand.

But the ambition is bigger than distribution. Kingdom Foods, Food Sock and LIF share a vision of increasing local sourcing, creating opportunities for Basotho businesses and building economic value in Lesotho.

Our ambition is not just to sell products. It is to help build opportunities, skills and industry at home.

The partnership includes a commitment to establish a manufacturing plant within one year of the launch of Phase One of the reseller programme, subject to the applicable implementation requirements. The planned facility is intended to create jobs, transfer skills and help keep more value within Lesotho.

Local farmers, suppliers, entrepreneurs and resellers can all play a role in the journey. By building participation today, we aim to help create the foundations for a stronger productive economy tomorrow.

Our guiding ambition is simple: products by Basotho, for Basotho and the world, with local impact at the heart of the journey.

The Future We Are Working Towards

A Manufacturing Future Built in Lesotho

LIF made the case for a partnership that can go beyond distribution and help establish industry in Lesotho. The planned manufacturing facility represents a future ambition for local value creation.

  • Jobs: create employment opportunities for Basotho.
  • Skills: develop practical production and operational experience.
  • Local value: build stronger links with local farmers and suppliers where feasible.
  • Opportunity: connect resellers and entrepreneurs to the wider journey.

The manufacturing plant is a future commitment, not an existing operational factory. Timing and delivery remain subject to implementation and applicable requirements.
