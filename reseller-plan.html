Rally Behind Food Sovereignty

The LIF Reseller Programme is designed to grow local participation in the distribution of food products and create pathways for individuals, families, groups and established businesses.

Whether you are starting small, supporting your household, organising a community group or operating a larger distribution business, the programme provides a pathway to discuss with LIF.

Our wider ambition is to strengthen local enterprise, expand access to products and help build a stronger foundation for food production and manufacturing in Lesotho.

Different starting points. One shared purpose.

Resellers can help connect products with customers through personal networks, neighbourhood sales, community organisations, retail outlets and broader distribution channels.

Take the first step: contact LIF to discuss the programme and the pathway that may suit your circumstances.

01 · StartDiscuss your interest and the type of reseller activity you want to build.
02 · BuildUnderstand the approved products, requirements and support available for your pathway.
03 · GrowDevelop customer relationships and explore opportunities to expand responsibly.

Five Reseller Pathways

The programme recognises that people and businesses have different capacities, networks and ambitions. Discuss the appropriate pathway directly with LIF.

Side Hustle

A starting pathway for an individual who wants to introduce products to customers alongside other commitments.

Family Provider

A pathway for someone looking to build a more organised household-focused selling activity.

Community Champion

A pathway for community-minded people and groups with the ability to connect with a wider local network.

Master Distributor

A pathway for experienced operators interested in coordinating distribution across a broader area.

Commercial Track

A pathway for established or developing businesses seeking to participate at a commercial distribution level.

Important programme information: This public page introduces the pathways only. Product allocations, starter quantities, prices, reseller margins and eligibility requirements are managed through LIF's approved programme information and are not published here. Please confirm the current details with LIF before making a commitment.

How to Take the Next Step

Start with a conversation so that LIF can understand your interest and explain the relevant process.

1. Contact LIF

Send an enquiry with your name, location and a short description of the reseller opportunity you are interested in.

2. Discuss the pathway

Ask LIF to explain the approved products, requirements, quantities and applicable commercial details.

3. Follow the official process

Proceed with registration only after receiving the current instructions and confirming the terms directly with LIF.
