Kingdom Foods

Food solutions for feeding at scale.

Kingdom Foods describes its work as manufacturing scalable food solutions, including VitaKidz, Maxi Blend and JOY Bucket. The public information below is a high-level product overview; detailed nutrient claims are not added unless supported by a specific product label.

VitaKidz

A nutrition-oriented product developed for children. Kingdom Foods states that it can be consumed as a meal or shake by adding water or milk. Exact age suitability, preparation, ingredients, allergens and nutrition must be taken from the current pack.

Maxi Blend

A bulk meal solution. Kingdom Foods describes preparation with boiling water and cooking for approximately 30–40 minutes. Confirm the product label for quantities, ingredients, allergens and nutrition.

JOY Bucket

A relief package intended to provide essential support to families during crises. It is a package of emergency relief items, rather than a single meal with one nutrition panel.

Kingdom Foods product descriptions above are based on publicly described product purposes. This page does not reproduce an unverified nutrition table for VitaKidz or Maxi Blend. Use the exact current product packaging for nutrition, ingredient, allergen, age suitability and preparation data.

Our wider mission

Products are one part of the journey.

LIF's ambition is to connect products, resellers, community needs and future local manufacturing. The proposed manufacturing plant is a future commitment and vision—not a claim that the plant is already operating. Our goal is to support local skills, jobs and more value remaining in Lesotho.
