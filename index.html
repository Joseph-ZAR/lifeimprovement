Flagship project

Food Sock: food security meets enterprise.

Launching 5 November 2026. Phase One focuses on placing products for resale, building a reputable sales programme, capturing a market niche and creating excitement for future manufacturing.

01Market entry: place products for resale and build disciplined sales activity.
02Build excitement: turn real customer demand and reseller stories into commercial momentum.
03Build people: train entrepreneurs to sell, serve customers and manage stock.
04Build the network: grow from resellers toward distributors and community food hubs.
