The Founder's Vision

A better Lesotho for today's families and future generations.

Life Improvement Foundation is driven by the vision of Khauli Glennold Ramakatane, known as Glen Ramakatane, a Basotho entrepreneur, philanthropist and community leader whose working life has been shaped by enterprise, opportunity and the wellbeing of families.

The vision is bigger than helping people cope with poverty. It is about changing the conditions that keep people trapped in it: limited opportunities to earn, barriers to enterprise, a lack of productive work and too little value being created locally.

LIF seeks a future in which Basotho can participate as entrepreneurs, workers, producers, suppliers, distributors and business owners. People should not be treated only as recipients of assistance; they should have pathways to become contributors, creators and owners of economic opportunity.

End poverty, do not simply manage it.

That means working towards lasting solutions: productive participation, stronger local enterprise, practical skills, employment and the capacity to create value within Lesotho.

What Our Mandate Means in Practice

Poverty must become a problem we work to solve, not a condition we learn to accept.

LIF's approach is to connect people with practical pathways towards greater self-reliance. Immediate needs matter, but lasting change also requires the ability to earn, produce, build skills and participate in a productive economy.

01

Create economic opportunity

Support pathways for people to participate in enterprise, distribution, retail, supply and other productive activities. Opportunity should be accessible to people at different stages of their economic journey.

02

Build skills and productive capacity

Work towards a future in which Basotho can gain experience, develop practical skills, take on responsibility and contribute to stronger businesses and industries.

03

Keep more value in Lesotho

Move beyond being only a destination for finished products. Build connections between local businesses, farmers, suppliers, workers and a future manufacturing base.

Our measure of progress: more people able to earn, more local businesses able to grow, more skills developed, more productive opportunities created and more value retained in Lesotho.

How the Idea Took Shape

A national ambition became a practical partnership.

LIF's mandate raised a fundamental question: how can a partnership help Basotho move from simply buying and distributing products towards participating in production, employment and local economic development?

The answer was to connect three complementary roles. LIF brought the Lesotho market-development and local-opportunity vision. Food Sock brought its product brands, intellectual property and commercial expertise. Kingdom Foods brought an agreed manufacturing role and complementary food products.

The purpose is not simply to sell products. It is to use market development as an early step towards a wider productive economy in which local suppliers, farmers, resellers, workers and entrepreneurs can have a meaningful place.

The Manufacturing Vision

From distribution to production. From opportunity to lasting value.

LIF wants the market created through this partnership to help lay the groundwork for a manufacturing plant in Lesotho. The vision is to create more productive opportunities, develop skills and connect suitable local resources to future production.

A future development goal

A Plant Built Around Opportunity

The partnership carries a commitment to establish a manufacturing plant within one year of launching Phase One of the reseller programme. Planning, readiness and agreed implementation arrangements remain important to delivery.
