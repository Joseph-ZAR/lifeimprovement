The vision behind LIF

Opportunity, dignity and self-reliance.

Life Improvement Foundation seeks to connect Basotho to opportunities to work, trade, build enterprises and take part in a stronger local economy.

“They want to work. They want to build a better life for their families.”

The Foundation’s approach is practical: introduce products, develop reseller opportunities, build partnerships and work towards a future manufacturing capability.

The journey to the manufacturing plant

From our land to our table.

Our long-term vision is to connect local resources, skills, processing, packaging and distribution — creating opportunities for Basotho along the way.
