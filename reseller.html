More Than Selling Food

A reseller model designed for real life in Lesotho.

Formal employment opportunities are limited, but families need income and communities need ways to create their own opportunities. The LIF reseller model is designed to connect those needs with practical enterprise.

You do not need to begin with a large shop. The model is intended to make it possible for a student, a parent, a market trader, a church member or an aspiring entrepreneur to start small and develop their business over time.

One person. One opportunity.

Your Starting Point

How the reseller model works

The foundation is straightforward: get started, introduce products to customers, earn through sales and build your business as you gain experience.

01

Choose your starting point

Begin with a Starter package containing products and tools intended to help you start trading.

02

Find your customers

Introduce the products to households, workplaces, community networks, local traders and other suitable customers.

03

Earn and reinvest

Buy at the applicable partner price, sell at the recommended price and retain the difference. Use experience and customer feedback to guide growth.

04

Build towards the future

Develop your business, earn points under the programme and progress towards higher reseller levels and potential future opportunities.

A business opportunity, not a promise of instant wealth

Reselling requires effort, customer service, responsible stock management and an understanding of local demand. Income depends on actual sales, costs and business activity. LIF's goal is to provide a structured pathway for participation and growth, not to guarantee a particular income or business outcome.

Recognition for Participation

A points system designed to reward growth

Points are an important part of the reseller model. They connect day-to-day trading with the opportunity to progress over time.

Benefit One

Discounts on future orders

Points earned through qualifying programme activity can be used towards discounts on subsequent orders. As your activity grows, points can help reduce the cost of restocking under the programme rules.

Reward your continued activity

The more consistently you participate, the more opportunities you may have to benefit from the programme's discount mechanism.

Benefit Two

Lifetime points and the ownership pathway

Lifetime points are tracked separately from points redeemed for discounts. Under the model described in the reseller brief, lifetime points do not decrease when discounts are redeemed.

A pathway towards a share-purchase invitation

Resellers who reach the programme's qualification threshold are intended to receive the first invitation to purchase shares in the planned manufacturing plant when it is established.

Points are subject to the programme rules. A share-purchase invitation is not a guarantee of shares, investment returns or an automatic ownership interest. Eligibility, availability and the applicable terms will need to be confirmed through the relevant arrangements.

Growing Beyond Individual Sales

The Community Food Hub

The reseller brief describes the Community Food Hub as a longer-term expression of the model: a local anchor that can sell directly to consumers, supply surrounding informal retailers, serve feeding schemes and create local employment.

A hub is more than a shop. The ambition is to build a dependable community business that keeps more economic activity circulating locally.

  • Serve local households Make products available to consumers in the community.
  • Support informal retailers Develop supply relationships with nearby vendors and small traders.
  • Contribute to feeding programmes Explore opportunities to supply suitable institutional and community feeding needs.
  • Build local employment Create opportunities as community enterprise develops.
Rooted in Community

Growing through trusted local structures

Life Improvement Foundation's model includes working through non-profit organisations that already have relationships within communities.

Connecting enterprise with community support

Community-based NPO partners can help identify aspiring entrepreneurs, provide suitable spaces for training and support participants as they develop. This approach is intended to connect business opportunity with the local relationships and knowledge that communities already have.

A local sourcing commitment

The reseller brief states that, once manufacturing begins in Lesotho, at least 70% of all ingredients will be locally sourced. This is a commitment for the planned manufacturing phase, not a claim that the future factory is already operating or that all current products are locally made.
