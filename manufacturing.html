A FUTURE VISION FOR LOCAL INDUSTRY AND JOB CREATION
A bigger purpose

Manufacturing means more than a building.

For Life Improvement Foundation, the manufacturing vision is about building productive capacity in Lesotho. It is about connecting local agricultural suppliers, food processing, packaging and distribution to a stronger local economy.

It is also about people: workers who can gain skills, farmers who can access market opportunities, entrepreneurs who can build businesses and families who can benefit when more economic activity takes place at home.

“Products today. Production tomorrow. More opportunities for Basotho.”
LOCAL SOURCING Build relationships with Basotho farmers and suppliers where products, quality and production requirements allow.
SKILLS DEVELOPMENT Create pathways to practical experience in food production, quality, packaging and logistics.
JOB CREATION Work toward future opportunities in production, warehousing, quality control, maintenance and distribution.
VALUE IN LESOTHO Grow local processing and keep more of the value chain connected to the national economy.

From farm to finished product

A journey that can create opportunity at every stage.

The proposed production ecosystem connects the people who grow raw materials with the people who process, package, move and sell finished products.

01

Farmers & suppliers

Develop reliable routes to market for suitable local agricultural produce and supplies.

02

Processing

Receive, inspect and process ingredients under planned food-safety and quality systems.

03

Packaging

Develop local packaging and product-preparation capabilities where feasible.

04

Quality & storage

Build quality checks and organized storage into the intended operating model.

05

Distribution

Connect products to resellers, retailers, institutions and wider markets.
