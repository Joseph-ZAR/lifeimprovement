The Journey to the Manufacturing Plant.

We want to build more than a distribution network. Our vision is to help create a future in which products can increasingly be sourced, produced and packaged in Lesotho — opening doors to jobs, practical skills, local enterprise and a stronger economy for Basotho.

Why manufacturing matters

From selling products to building productive capacity.

Life Improvement Foundation's manufacturing vision grows from the Food Sock, Two Spoons and Kingdom Foods partnership and the market-development work being built in Lesotho.

The journey begins with products reaching customers and a reseller network developing. Over time, the ambition is to connect that market to local sourcing, food processing, packaging and manufacturing capacity.

This is about creating pathways for Basotho farmers, suppliers, workers and entrepreneurs to participate in more stages of the value chain — rather than relying only on finished products manufactured elsewhere.

70% Local sourcing ambition
Products by Basotho

More local resources. More local participation.

Our ambition is to work towards sourcing approximately 70% of suitable ingredients, materials and resources locally where feasible as manufacturing develops.

This approach aims to create stronger connections with Basotho farmers, producers, suppliers and businesses while developing a supply chain that can support local production.

The 70% figure is an ambition, not a claim about current sourcing. Actual sourcing will depend on product requirements, quality standards, availability, cost and reliable supply.
From market to manufacturing

Every stage of the journey matters.

Building a manufacturing ecosystem takes a connected effort: customers, resellers, suppliers, technical partners and local production all have a role to play.

01

Develop the market

Introduce Food Sock, Two Spoons and Kingdom Foods products to customers and strengthen product awareness in Lesotho.

02

Grow the reseller network

Build customer relationships, distribution reach and a network of local entrepreneurs serving their communities.

03

Connect local suppliers

Identify suitable Basotho farmers, producers and suppliers who can meet future product and production requirements.

04

Prepare for production

Progress the planning, agreements, technical requirements, quality systems and resources needed for local manufacturing.

05

Build local capacity

Work towards production and packaging in Lesotho, with potential opportunities for jobs, skills and local enterprise.

Why the reseller programme matters

A market today can help prepare the ground for production tomorrow.

Resellers are part of a wider market-development effort. Customers, local distribution networks and commercial partners help establish the reach that future local production would need.

↗

Build demand

A growing customer base and organised distribution network can help establish a clearer understanding of market needs and product demand.

◎

Connect people and partners

Resellers, local businesses, suppliers and potential implementation partners can contribute to a stronger commercial network around the initiative.

⌂

Prepare for local production

Market development can inform planning for suitable production, local sourcing, packaging, storage and distribution as the manufacturing ambition advances.

One connected journey: LIF's reseller programme is part of the wider market-development effort. The manufacturing ambition will also depend on the relevant agreements, resources, technical planning and implementation. Participation in the reseller programme does not guarantee investment returns, factory ownership or shares.
