A bigger purpose

Manufacturing means more than a building.

For Life Improvement Foundation, the manufacturing vision is about building productive capacity in Lesotho. It is about connecting local agricultural suppliers, food processing, packaging and distribution to a stronger local economy.

It is also about people: the workers who gain skills, the farmers who gain market opportunities, the entrepreneurs who build businesses and the families who benefit when more economic activity takes place at home.

“Products today. Production tomorrow. More opportunities for Basotho.”

From farm to finished product

A journey that can create opportunity at every stage.

The proposed production ecosystem connects the people who grow raw materials with the people who process, package, move and sell finished products.

01

Farmers

Build reliable routes to market for local agricultural produce and suppliers.

02

Processing

Receive, inspect and process suitable ingredients under planned food-safety systems.

03

Packaging

Develop local packaging and product-preparation capabilities where feasible.

04

Quality & storage

Build quality checks and organized storage into the intended operating model.

05

Distribution

Connect products to resellers, retailers, institutions and wider markets.
